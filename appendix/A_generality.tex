\subsubsection{Proof of Lemma~\ref{lem:multiplicative-tsgd-tuned-exact}}
\label{app:proof-multiplicative-tsgd-tuned}

Fix $K\geq2$ and run TSGD with $\eta=\eta_K^\star$. For the
multiplicative oracle, the updates and the tracking error
$e_k=g_k-x_k$ satisfy
\[
g_{k+1}=(1-\eta a_{k+1})g_k,
\qquad
e_{k+1}=e_k+\eta(1-a_{k+1})g_k.
\]
Using $\mathbb E[a]=1$ and $\mathbb E[(1-a)^2]=1$, define
$M_k=\mathbb E[g_k^2]$, $E_k=\mathbb E[e_k^2]$, and
$C_k=\mathbb E[g_ke_k]$. Independence of the fresh sample gives
\[
M_{k+1}=qM_k,\qquad
E_{k+1}=E_k+\eta^2M_k,\qquad
C_{k+1}=(1-\eta)C_k+\eta^2M_k,
\]
where $q=\mathbb E[(1-\eta a)^2]=1-2\eta+2\eta^2$.
Since $M_0=1$ and $E_0=C_0=0$, solving the first two recursions
gives
\[
M_k=q^k,\qquad
E_k=\frac{\eta}{2(1-\eta)}(1-q^k),
\]
and iterating the cross-moment recursion gives
\[
C_k=\eta^2\sum_{t=0}^{k-1}(1-\eta)^{k-1-t}q^t.
\]
Because all summands are nonnegative, extending the resulting
finite double sum to all nonnegative indices yields
\[
\sum_{k<K}C_k
\leq\eta^2\left(\sum_{t\geq0}q^t\right)
             \left(\sum_{j\geq0}(1-\eta)^j\right)
=\frac{\eta^2}{(1-q)\eta}
=\frac{1}{2(1-\eta)},
\]
where $1-q=2\eta(1-\eta)$. Hence
$K^{-1}\sum_{k<K}C_k=O(K^{-1})$, since $\eta_K^\star\to0$.

For the remaining moments, geometric summation gives
\[
\frac1K\sum_{k<K}M_k=\frac{1-q^K}{K(1-q)},\qquad
\frac1K\sum_{k<K}E_k=
\frac{\eta}{2(1-\eta)}
\left(1-\frac1K\sum_{k<K}M_k\right).
\]
Now $\eta_K^\star\sim K^{-1/2}$ and
$1-q=2\eta_K^\star(1-\eta_K^\star)\sim2K^{-1/2}$.
Moreover, $q^K\leq e^{-K(1-q)}\to0$, because
$K(1-q)\sim2\sqrt K$. Therefore
\[
\frac1K\sum_{k<K}M_k\sim\frac{1}{2\sqrt K},
\qquad
\frac1K\sum_{k<K}E_k\sim\frac{1}{2\sqrt K}.
\]
Finally, $x_k=g_k-e_k$, so
$G_k^{\mathrm{TSGD}}=\mathbb E[x_k^2]=M_k+E_k-2C_k$.
The averaged cross moment is lower order, and thus
$\sqrt K\,\overline G_K^{\mathrm{TSGD}}\to1$, as claimed.
