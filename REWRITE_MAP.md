## Technical preservation

- Appendix A retains the full descent inequality, weighted accumulation identity,
  terminal objective and estimator-norm terms, exact optimized stepsizes,
  constant-step and tuned SGD bounds, and iteration-complexity guarantees.
- Appendix B retains the four quadratic cases, general exact-transport and
  tilted-objective identities, the lower-boundedness qualification for the
  tilted objective, explicit trajectories, mean-square convergence, false
  convergence, the conservation law, interaction formula, and initialization
  coefficient sharpness.
- Appendix C retains both global-noise-assumption counterexamples, the log-cosh
  guarantee without BV, the qualified SGD second-moment divergence calculation,
  the spectral finite-sum similarity formula, and the example with delta larger
  than the average-objective smoothness constant.
- Appendix D retains the additive and multiplicative exact dynamics, asymptotic
  sharpness calculations, the tuned-TSGD lemma and its original proof, and the
  sinusoidal-noise identities. It also documents the new computed figure.
- Appendix E retains initialization costs, equal-iteration and equal-oracle
  comparisons of upper bounds, restart/refresh recursions, compression details,
  MVR noise covariance, MARS and VENUS identities, and related special cases.

All previous labeled equations remain identifiable by their existing labels,
except `eq:sgd-update`, whose identical iterate update is now shared by all three
methods under `eq:tsgd-iterate`. Section and equation numbers change with the new
organization. The old placeholder appendix title and empty subsections are
removed; the source snapshot retains the entire previous version.

## New numerical material

The sinusoidal experiment is generated by `scripts/similarity_experiment.py`.
It uses actual seeded simulations with 100,000 trajectories per curve. Both SGD
and TSGD receive the same exact initial estimate through a charged two-call
antithetic construction available for this oracle. Subsequent SGD steps cost
one evaluation and coupled TSGD steps cost two. The script verifies the known
SGD recurrence and exact-transport trajectory and retains all simulated tails.
CSV means/standard errors and JSON metadata accompany the plotted figure.
