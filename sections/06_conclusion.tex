\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Transported SGD is a fundamental way to use stochastic-gradient
information: it estimates changes and carries an existing estimate
along the trajectory. With a sufficiently accurate initial estimate,
small variation in gradient noise can make transport effective even
when individual gradients are very noisy. Its performance depends on
the initialization error it retains and the transport errors it
accumulates. The recursion is already studied on its own in methods
such as SARAH-IN, and also appears within more elaborate optimizers.
Our presentation emphasizes its role as a fundamental estimator and
makes its two error sources explicit. We also briefly discuss reasons
for combining transport with fresh estimation and momentum averaging.
